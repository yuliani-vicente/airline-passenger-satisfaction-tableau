# Airline Passenger Satisfaction Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 129,880 airline passenger records to identify factors associated with customer satisfaction. I created an interactive dashboard in Tableau Public that summarizes overall satisfaction, flight delays, passenger class, travel type, and customer type.

## Business Questions

* What percentage of passengers were satisfied?
* Which passenger class had the highest satisfaction rate?
* Are business travelers more satisfied than personal travelers?
* Are returning customers more satisfied than first-time customers?
* How do arrival delays differ between satisfied and dissatisfied passengers?

## Tools and Skills

* Tableau Public
* Data visualization
* Calculated fields
* Exploratory data analysis

## Dashboard Metrics

* **Total Passengers:** 129,880
* **Overall Satisfaction Rate:** 43.4%
* **Average Arrival Delay:** 15.09 minutes
* **Average Departure Delay:** 14.71 minutes

## Key Findings

* Business-class passengers had the highest satisfaction rate at 69.4%.
* Business travelers had a 58.4% satisfaction rate, compared with 10.1% for personal travelers.
* Returning customers had a 47.8% satisfaction rate, compared with 24.0% for first-time customers.
* Neutral or dissatisfied passengers experienced an average arrival delay of 17.06 minutes, compared with 12.53 minutes for satisfied passengers.
* The results suggest that passenger class, travel purpose, customer loyalty, and delays may be associated with satisfaction.
